# HOODCITY

A Robin Hood themed first-person 3D trading minigame built with Three.js. Explore a medieval market town, visit realistic shops, and trade goods with merchants and wandering travelers.

## Play

```bash
cd hoodcity
npm start
```

Then open http://localhost:3000 in your browser.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| WASD | Move |
| Mouse | Look around |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Space | Jump |
| E | Trade with nearby merchant/traveler |
| Tab | Open inventory |
| Escape | Close menus |

## Features

- **First-person Robin Hood character** — See your bow, gloves, hood, and quiver
- **5 realistic medieval shops** — Archery, Blacksmith, Tavern, Market, Treasury
- **Trading system** — Buy and sell items with gold, or barter with travelers
- **Wandering NPCs** — Trade items with travelers roaming the town square
- **Detailed town** — Cobblestone streets, forest, fountain, torches, timber-frame buildings

## Shops

- **Will Scarlet's Archery** — Bows, arrows, cloaks
- **Little John's Forge** — Swords, armor, iron goods
- **The Merry Outlaw Tavern** — Food, ale, potions
- **Sherwood Market Stall** — General goods and treasures
- **Robin's Treasury** — Rare items and legendary treasures
